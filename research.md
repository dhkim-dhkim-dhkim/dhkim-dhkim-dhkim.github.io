# Research

My research focuses on computational approaches to the design
and understanding of photonic systems.


## Inverse-Designed Nanophotonics

I develop computational frameworks based on electromagnetic simulation,
adjoint optimization, and topology optimization to design nanophotonic
devices with targeted optical functionalities.


## Light–Matter Interactions

I am interested in enhancing and controlling light–matter interactions
through engineered nanophotonic structures, including systems involving
emerging and quantum materials.


## Integrated Photonics

I am interested in extending inverse-design techniques toward compact
and scalable photonic integrated circuits.
