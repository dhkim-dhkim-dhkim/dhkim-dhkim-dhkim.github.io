    <h1>Research</h1>

    <p class="research-lead">
      My research explores computational approaches to photonic device design,
      with a focus on inverse design, light–matter interactions, and integrated
      photonics. The topics below represent both my current projects and
      research directions I am interested in pursuing.
    </p>

  </header>

      <h2>
        Computational Imaging &amp; Spectral Routing
      </h2>

    <div class="research-project-grid">


      <article class="research-project">

        <h3>
          Nanophotonic Color Routers for CMOS Image Sensors
        </h3>

        <p>
          Inverse-designed nanophotonic color routers for compact CMOS image
          sensors, with an emphasis on scalable full-sensor-plane integration,
          suppression of stitching errors, and fabrication-aware design.
        </p>

        <div class="research-keywords">
          <span>Color Routing</span>
          <span>Inverse Design</span>
          <span>CMOS Imaging</span>
        </div>

      </article>


      <article class="research-project">

        <h3>
          Hyperspectral Routers for Snapshot Imaging
        </h3>

        <p>
          Multichannel spectral routing structures for compact snapshot
          hyperspectral imaging, with an interest in scalability, channel
          count, and device-level performance.
        </p>

        <div class="research-keywords">
          <span>Hyperspectral Imaging</span>
          <span>Spectral Routing</span>
          <span>Nanophotonics</span>
        </div>

      </article>


    </div>

      <h2>
        Light–Matter Interaction Engineering
      </h2>

    </div>


    <div class="research-project-grid">


      <article class="research-project">

        <h3>
          Inverse Design of Nonlinear Nanophotonic Devices
        </h3>

        <p>
          Inverse-designed nanophotonic structures that leverage nonlinear
          optical materials and resonant field enhancement for efficient
          nonlinear light–matter interactions and frequency conversion.
        </p>

        <div class="research-keywords">
          <span>Nonlinear Photonics</span>
          <span>Inverse Design</span>
          <span>Frequency Conversion</span>
        </div>

      </article>


      <article class="research-project">

        <h3>
          Light–Matter Interaction Engineering in Topological Quantum Materials
        </h3>

        <p>
          Engineering the optical and thermal environments of topological
          quantum materials to enhance and control photoresponses, including
          optical–thermal co-design for photo-thermoelectric response
          engineering.
        </p>

        <div class="research-keywords">
          <span>Quantum Materials</span>
          <span>Light–Matter Interaction</span>
          <span>Optical–Thermal Co-design</span>
        </div>

      </article>


    </div>

    <div class="research-project-grid">


      <article class="research-project">

        <h3>
          Inverse-Designed Devices for Photonic Crystal Waveguides
        </h3>

        <p>
          Compact passive photonic components for photonic-crystal waveguide
          platforms using inverse design, with an emphasis on strong optical
          confinement and dense photonic integration.
        </p>

        <div class="research-keywords">
          <span>Photonic Crystals</span>
          <span>Integrated Photonics</span>
          <span>Passive Devices</span>
        </div>

      </article>


      <article class="research-project">

        <h3>
          Fabrication-Aware and Robust Inverse Design across Photonic Platforms
        </h3>

        <p>
          Robust inverse design of passive photonic devices across silicon,
          silicon nitride, and lithium niobate platforms, incorporating
          fabrication constraints, uncertainty, and platform-specific design
          requirements.
        </p>

        <div class="research-keywords">
          <span>Silicon</span>
          <span>Silicon Nitride</span>
          <span>Lithium Niobate</span>
